SPOTLIGHT ON SAVINGS

How the Martins Saved $12K on Insurance Premiums 💰

The Challenge: David and Sarah Martin, both 52, were paying over $18,000 annually across life, disability, and long-term care insurance — but hadn't reviewed their policies in 8 years.

Our Approach: We conducted a comprehensive coverage audit, identified $400K in redundant life insurance overlap, and restructured their disability policy to reflect their current income.

The Result: The Martins now save $12,200/year while maintaining the same level of protection — and redirected those savings into a Roth IRA for tax-free retirement growth.
