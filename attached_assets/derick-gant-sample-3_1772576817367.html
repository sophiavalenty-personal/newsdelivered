From $65K in Debt to $8K in Savings

Husband and wife, high income earners, $65,000 in credit card debt and NO savings.

Two young boys under 6 years of age.

Mom and Dad started working with me 10 months ago. They’ve paid down $25,000 in debt and now have $8,000 in savings!

No longer living paycheck to paycheck. Huge gamechanger for them!

 

◆  Takeaways  ◆

◆ High Income Doesn’t Equal Financial Security
◆ Small, Consistent Changes Create Big Results
◆ A Plan Brings Peace of Mind

Your Next Steps
01 Stop the Bleeding
Commit to no new credit card debt starting today. If needed, remove cards from your wallet or online accounts.
02 Quick Debt Count
Add up your debt — write down the number. You can’t fix what you haven’t measured.
03 Redirect One Monthly Expense
Identify one recurring expense to reduce or eliminate. Redirect that amount toward debt payoff or savings.
